# About CES

## What is CES?

The **Cairns Engineering Society (CES)** is the student-run club for engineering students at James Cook University's Cairns campus. CES is an affiliated club of the James Cook University Student Association (JCUSA).

Our objectives, from the [CES constitution](files/CES-Constitution.pdf), are:

1. To build a social community for engineering students studying at JCU Cairns.
2. To encourage the practice of the engineering disciplines offered on the Cairns campus.
3. To build industry links that support the career development of our members.

## What we do

- **Social events:** BBQs, movie and pizza nights, bowling and roller skating, and end of trimester events. See the [events page](events.md).
- **Student information:** info nights and this website, where senior students share what they wish they'd known.
- **Industry links:** connecting students with local engineers and employers.

## How to join

Find any CES committee member (at an event, on campus or on [Discord](https://discord.gg/rsNMRNq7uh)) and pay the **$10 membership fee**. Cash and card are both accepted.

- Membership runs for a year, from the start of Trimester 1 to the start of Trimester 1 the following year.
- Members get perks at events. For example, a free can of soft drink with your BBQ burger.
- You don't have to be a member to come to most events.

**Who can join?** Under the constitution there are three types of membership:

- **Undergraduate:** currently enrolled in an approved undergraduate course at JCU Cairns. Engineering is automatically approved.
- **Alumni:** graduates of a JCU Cairns engineering course.
- **Associate:** anyone invited by the committee, or accepted by the committee after asking to join.

CES is also listed on the [JCUSA Cairns clubs page](https://www.jcusa.edu.au/clubs/join-a-club-cairns/).

## The committee

CES is run by an **executive** elected each year, plus any members who join the wider **committee** to help out.

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| **President** | The spokesperson for CES. Represents the club, leads reaffiliation with JCUSA, and organises events with the Vice President and Secretary. |
| **Vice President** | Stands in for the President when needed and helps organise events. |
| **Secretary & Industry Liaison** | Keeps meeting minutes and the member register, handles club correspondence, and is the first point of contact for industry partnerships. |
| **Treasurer** | Manages the club's money: keeps records of everything received and spent, and reports the accounts to the committee and the AGM. |

Want to know who's currently in each role? Ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/rsNMRNq7uh) or [email us](mailto:cairns.engineering.society@gmail.com).

## How to get involved

- **Come to an event.** Everyone's welcome. See the [events page](events.md) and the [calendar](index.md#calendar).
- **Join the committee.** Any CES member can ask to join the wider committee and help run events. Just talk to an executive member.
- **Run for the executive.** The executive is elected at the **Annual General Meeting (AGM)**, held around **week 7 of the final trimester** each year. To be eligible, you must:
  - be a CES member,
  - have completed at least 2 subjects of the first-year engineering program,
  - have been enrolled at JCU for more than 12 months when you take on the role, and
  - **not** be in your final year of study while in the role.
- **Count it towards placement.** Running events as an office-bearer, or volunteering significantly with CES, can count towards up to 3 weeks of your placement requirement. See the [placement guide](placement.md#what-counts-towards-your-hours).
- **Help with this website.** Review a subject, share a tip or [send us a suggestion](https://forms.gle/n4fg7VV2BTVFQaYu6).
