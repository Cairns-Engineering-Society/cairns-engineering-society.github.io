# Subjects

What are the subjects actually like? These pages are written from **past students' experience** to give you an idea of what to expect: the workload, the style of assessment, and what people wish they'd known.

> **This is student opinion, not official information.** For official details (prerequisites, credit points, assessment weightings), always check the JCU handbook link given for each subject.

## How the subject pages work

- Each year page lists that year's subjects, grouped by major: **Shared** (everyone takes it), **Electrical**, **IoT** and **Mechanical**.
- Click a subject's name to open it.
- Each subject has a **"Taken by:"** line showing who takes it. For example, the **Mechanical minor** uses the same subjects as the **Mechanical core**, so those subjects are written once and labelled for both.
- **Dual degrees** (e.g. engineering with science or IT) aren't covered yet.
