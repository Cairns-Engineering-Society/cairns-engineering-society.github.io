# Subjects

What are the subjects actually like? These pages are written from **past students' experience** to give you an idea of what to expect: the workload, the style of assessment, and what people wish they'd known.

> **This is student opinion, not official information.** For official details (prerequisites, credit points, assessment weightings), always check the JCU handbook link given for each subject. Subject lists and study periods are from the **2027 JCU handbook**.

## Pick a level

- [Level 1 subjects](level-1.md): codes starting with 1, mostly first year
- [Level 2 subjects](level-2.md): codes starting with 2
- [Level 3 subjects](level-3.md): codes starting with 3
- [Level 4 subjects](level-4.md): codes starting with 4, including the Honours thesis

**Why levels, not years?** A subject's level is the first digit of its code (EG**2**008 is level 2), and it doesn't always match your year. For example, some level 3 subjects are taken in 2nd year. To see which subjects you take when, check your course plan in the [JCU handbook](https://handbook.jcu.edu.au/course/2027/116209) or [Your degree explained](../degree.md).

## How the subject pages work

- **Click a subject's name** to open it.
- **Subjects are grouped by who takes them:** Core (all majors), Electrical and IoT, Electrical only, IoT only, Mechanical, Maritime, and so on. Each subject is listed **once**.
- **The "Taken by:" line** shows every major and minor that includes the subject. For example, the **Mechatronics minor** (popular with Electrical and IoT students) mostly uses Mechanical core subjects, so those subjects say "Mechatronics minor" on their Taken by line.
- **"When"** shows the study period the subject runs in Cairns, from the 2027 handbook.
- **Maritime** has its own group because of extra conditions: only the first two years are in Cairns, then you move to Tasmania.
- **Other minors** (Data Science, Infrastructure and Environment, Mathematics, Software) and **double degrees** aren't covered yet. See the handbook pages for the [Data Science](https://handbook.jcu.edu.au/aos/2027/BENDSCMIN), [Infrastructure and Environment](https://handbook.jcu.edu.au/aos/2027/BENIPLMIN), [Mathematics](https://handbook.jcu.edu.au/aos/2027/BENMATMIN), [Mechatronics](https://handbook.jcu.edu.au/aos/2027/BENMCHMIN) and [Software](https://handbook.jcu.edu.au/aos/2027/BENSOFMIN) minors.
