## Study

- [LearnJCU](https://www.jcu.edu.au/learn): subject sites, lecture recordings and assignments. Also has the **Engineering: Work Integrated Learning Including Professional Practice** organisation for placement information.
- [JCU handbook](https://handbook.jcu.edu.au/): official subject details, prerequisites and assessment. Start with the [Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) 2027](https://handbook.jcu.edu.au/course/2027/116209) course page.
- [Timetables and important dates](https://www.jcu.edu.au/students/academic-calendars): academic calendars, census dates and your class timetable
- [The Learning Centre](https://www.jcu.edu.au/students/learning-centre): help with study skills, writing and maths
- [Student IT essentials](https://www.jcu.edu.au/information-and-communications-technology/help-and-support/student-it-essentials): logins, Wi-Fi, software and IT help

## Enrolment and admin

- [Enrolment](https://www.jcu.edu.au/students/enrolment): enrolling, changing subjects and course planning
- **Changing your major or degree?** Talk to the course coordinator first, then email [enrolments@jcu.edu.au](mailto:enrolments@jcu.edu.au). See [Your degree explained](degree.md#changing-your-major-or-campus).
- [Assessment and results](https://www.jcu.edu.au/students/assessment-and-results): exams, results and assessment rules
- [Exam timetables](https://www.jcu.edu.au/students/assessment-and-results/formal-examinations): formal exam dates and rules
- [Special consideration](https://www.jcu.edu.au/students/assessment-and-results/special-consideration): extensions and deferred exams when something goes wrong
- [Contact JCU services](https://www.jcu.edu.au/students/student-life/jcu-contact-index): who to contact for what

## Placement and careers

- [Placement guide on this site](placement.md): the whole process, step by step
- [InPlace](https://inplace.jcu.edu.au/): JCU's placement system, where you register placements and find opportunities
- [JCU Student Placements](https://www.jcu.edu.au/placements): the Student Placements Team ([placements@jcu.edu.au](mailto:placements@jcu.edu.au))
- [JCU Careers and Employability](https://www.jcu.edu.au/careers): job board, resume help and career advice
- [Careers and jobs while studying](careers.md): PASS leader, mentor, ambassador and networking tips

## Money

- [Fees and financial support](https://www.jcu.edu.au/students/fees-and-financial-support): fees, payment options and financial help
- [Scholarships](https://www.jcu.edu.au/scholarships): JCU scholarships and how to apply

## Support

- [Student support](https://www.jcu.edu.au/students/support): all JCU student support services
- [Personal support](https://www.jcu.edu.au/students/support/personal-support): counselling and wellbeing
- [AccessAbility support](https://www.jcu.edu.au/health-wellbeing-belonging/accessability-at-jcu): adjustments for disability, health conditions and other circumstances
- [Indigenous student support](https://www.jcu.edu.au/ierc/current-students): support for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander students
- [Safety and wellbeing](https://www.jcu.edu.au/safety-and-wellbeing): staying safe on campus and reporting concerns

## Clubs and community

- [CES on Discord](https://discord.gg/rsNMRNq7uh): announcements, event polls and chat
- [CES LinkedIn group](https://www.linkedin.com/groups/14317025/)
- [JCUSA Cairns clubs](https://www.jcusa.edu.au/clubs/join-a-club-cairns/): CES and other JCU Cairns clubs

## Professional

- [Engineers Australia](https://www.engineersaustralia.org.au/): the professional body for engineers. Student membership and local events.
- [Australian Maritime College (UTAS) Maritime Engineering](https://www.utas.edu.au/study/undergraduate/maritime-engineering): for Maritime students moving to Tasmania
